01 · Story

A tradition older than the car itself.

For a thousand years, oud was reserved for diwans, weddings, and the quiet moments before prayer. It was never cheap. It was never rushed. And it was never bottled into the chemical sprays you find in service stations.

RIHLA began with a simple question: why should the most important hour of your day — the one spent alone, in motion — smell like anyone else's?

02 · Craft

What makes our oud different.

I

Sustainably harvested agarwood

We source exclusively from cultivated plantations in Assam, Trat, and Koh Kong. No wild poaching. Every tree replanted.

II

Hydro-distilled, never diluted

Our oils rest for a minimum of three years in clay before bottling. What you smell is the wood itself — smoke, leather, honey, rain.

III

Engineered for the cabin

A magnetic vent diffuser with calibrated airflow. Scent intensity is controlled by a single brass dial. No heat. No plastic. No sickly sweet undertones.

03 · Collection

Three journeys. One ritual.

Hind

Assam, India

Dark, resinous, and faintly sweet. Stewed figs, wet leather, old libraries.

Khmer

Koh Kong, Cambodia

Bright and woody. Cut cedar, sandalwood, a breath of eucalyptus.

Hijazi

Taif, Saudi Arabia

Warm rose and smoke. Bukhoor at dusk, cardamom coffee, desert air after rain.
