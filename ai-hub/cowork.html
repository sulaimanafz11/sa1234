Daily Workflow

Work alongside AI
every single day.

Not "use AI sometimes for ideas". A structured daily routine — inbox, calendar, meetings, research, writing, planning — with AI doing the bottom 60% so you can do the top 40% better.

The stack

A lean daily AI setup

  • One thinking partner — Claude (Project) or ChatGPT (Custom GPT). Has your context, voice, ongoing projects.
  • One research assistant — Perplexity for facts, NotebookLM for your own docs.
  • One meeting note-taker — Granola (Mac) or Fathom. Local-first preferred.
  • One automation backbone — n8n cloud or self-host. For inbox / repetitive flows.
  • One writing partner — same as your thinking partner, but with a "writing brand voice" system prompt.
  • One image tool — NanoBanana for fast, MJ for hero images.

Resist the urge to add a 7th tool until one of the above truly fails you. Tool sprawl kills the routine.

Daily routine

A real day with AI

Morning · 15 minutes

  • Inbox triage — n8n agent already classified overnight. You only see the "needs human" pile.
  • Calendar review — ask Claude/ChatGPT: "Look at today's meetings, suggest the one outcome I want from each."
  • Day plan — 3 must-do tasks. AI helps reorder by impact.

Meetings · throughout the day

  • Granola / Fathom captures & structures notes automatically.
  • After: paste transcript into Claude → "Pull decisions, action items (with owners), open questions."
  • Send the action items to attendees within 5 minutes of the call ending.

Deep work blocks · 90 minutes

  • Claude / ChatGPT in one window. Your work in the other.
  • Ask for plans before drafts. Drafts before final. Cuts revision loops in half.
  • If stuck: "Be my devil's advocate, list the 5 reasons this could fail."

Research blocks · 30 minutes

  • Perplexity for any factual question — gives sources.
  • NotebookLM for "deep dive into the docs / PDFs I've already collected".
  • Save findings into a Notion DB tagged by topic — your second brain compounds.

Wind-down · 10 minutes

  • Dump the day into Claude: "Here's what I did. What's the most important thing for tomorrow?"
  • Quick log into Notion. The act of writing it = reinforces it.
  • Close email. Phone in another room.
