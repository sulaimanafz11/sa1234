Your best-fit path

Path 1

AI-assisted freelance services

What it is: sell a service you already understand (writing, design, research, admin) and use AI to deliver it 5× faster. You charge per project — clients don't care that you used AI.

Starter offer ideas

  • Email rewriting / inbox cleanup — £30/hour
  • Blog writing — £150–£300 per 1,500-word piece
  • Research briefs — £80–£150 per brief
  • SEO meta + descriptions — £100 for 20 pages
  • Social captions in batch — £200 for 30 captions

First three steps

  1. Pick the niche you already know best (your day job adjacent is gold).
  2. Build 2 portfolio samples — do them for free for someone real, not made-up.
  3. List on Upwork / Fiverr / X / LinkedIn. Apply to 10 jobs daily for 2 weeks.

Tools: Claude / ChatGPT for drafting, Grammarly, Notion for client docs.

Path 2

Productised services

What it is: the same service, packaged with a fixed scope, fixed price, fixed turnaround. Clients click "Buy". You're not selling time, you're selling a thing.

Examples that work in 2026

  • "Brand voice guide in 7 days" — £499
  • "30 LinkedIn posts in your voice" — £299
  • "Custom GPT for your team" — £799
  • "AI inbox triage setup (n8n)" — £400 setup + £80/mo
  • "100 AI-generated product images" — £500

First three steps

  1. Define one tight outcome — single deliverable, single price.
  2. Build a one-page sales site (Carrd / Framer / Lovable) with: outcome, price, timeline, 1 case study.
  3. DM 50 specific people who'd benefit. Don't post. DM.
Path 3

Micro-SaaS / vibe-coded apps

What it is: a tiny software product solving one specific problem. £5–£30/mo. You don't need 10,000 users — 200 paying ones is a real income.

Real examples shipping in 2026

  • AI meeting-note formatter for a specific industry
  • Niche newsletter summariser ("get every fintech newsletter in one daily email")
  • Browser extension that rewrites your emails in your voice
  • Internal CRM-cleanup bot for small agencies

First three steps

  1. Find a Reddit thread or X post where someone says "I wish there was a tool that…"
  2. Build the smallest version with Lovable / Bolt / Claude Code. 1 week max.
  3. Charge from day 1. Even £5/mo. Free users don't tell you the truth.
Why now: AI coding tools have collapsed the build-cost. A solo person can ship a real SaaS in 2 weekends. The bottleneck is finding a real problem, not building.
Path 4

Content creator / faceless brand

What it is: build an audience around a niche topic (AI tools, productivity, faith, finance) and monetise via sponsorships, affiliate links, info products, or your own SaaS.

Faceless formats that work

  • YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels with AI voiceover (ElevenLabs) + AI b-roll (Veo / Runway)
  • Newsletter — pick a tight niche, ship 2× a week, 12 months minimum
  • Twitter / X thread account in a niche
  • Pinterest pin factory (still surprisingly profitable for affiliate)

First three steps

  1. Pick a niche tight enough that someone could describe your account in one sentence.
  2. Commit to 90 posts in 90 days. Brutal but necessary. Use AI to draft, you to edit.
  3. Add one monetisation path only after you cross 1,000 followers.
Path 5

Automation agency

What it is: build n8n / Make / Zapier workflows for businesses. They pay setup + retainer. The Nate Herk / Liam Ottley playbook.

Common builds

  • Lead-gen agent (sites scrape → AI qualify → CRM)
  • Customer support triage
  • Appointment scheduling + reminder agent
  • Content repurposing pipelines
  • Reporting dashboards (data → AI → weekly summary email)

Pricing

  • Setup: £1,000–£5,000 per workflow
  • Retainer: £500–£2,500/mo for ongoing iteration + monitoring

First three steps

  1. Master n8n — build 5 flows for free / yourself first.
  2. Pick a single industry (real estate / legal / e-com / coaches). Specialisation sells.
  3. Outreach: 20 cold DMs / day to that industry, with a specific workflow you'd build for them.
Path 6

Info products & cohorts

What it is: teach what you know. Course, ebook, cohort, paid newsletter, paid Discord. Higher margin than services once it's built.

Examples

  • Notion template pack — £19–£49 one-off
  • Course: "Build your first AI agent in 7 days" — £199
  • Cohort: "30-day automation agency launchpad" — £499–£1,500
  • Paid newsletter: £8–£20/mo

First three steps

  1. Don't make a course as your first product. Sell the service first, then teach what you learned.
  2. When you've delivered the service 5+ times, package it as a course.
  3. Pre-sell the course before building it. If 10 people don't buy the promise, no one will buy the course.
Path 7

Vibe-coded MVP for someone else

What it is: founders / consultants pay you to build their idea into a working web app using AI tools (Lovable / Bolt / Cursor / Claude Code). Faster than traditional dev shops. £2k–£15k per MVP.

Why it works in 2026

  • Non-technical founders see AI demos and want one for their idea — they need a builder.
  • Traditional agencies still quote £20k+ for an MVP. You can do it for £5k in 2 weeks.
  • You're not selling cheap dev — you're selling speed.

First three steps

  1. Build 2 sample MVPs (yours, not for clients) and post the build in public.
  2. Reach out to 30 founders / coaches / consultants on LinkedIn with a specific MVP idea for their business.
  3. Charge a fixed fee for a 14-day sprint. Clear scope. Clear deliverable.
Frameworks

Two ideas that compound

Buy back your time (Dan Martell)

List the things you do daily. For each, label it $10/hr, $50/hr, $500/hr, $5,000/hr work. Use AI + automations + (later) a VA to remove the bottom-end work first. Reinvest the freed time into higher-tier work. Compounds fast.

Tasks AI eats first

  • Email triage & templated replies
  • Drafting initial versions of any content
  • Meeting notes & summaries
  • Research briefs
  • Social captions
  • Data cleanup / formatting

The agency stack

If you go the services / agency route, the leanest 2026 stack:

  • Outreach: Apollo / Lemlist / cold DMs by hand
  • CRM: Notion or Airtable. Don't pay for HubSpot until you're at £20k MRR.
  • Delivery: n8n + Claude API + Google Workspace
  • Onboarding: Tally / Typeform forms → automated Notion docs
  • Invoicing: Stripe Invoices or Wise
  • Comms: Slack Connect with each client

Outreach scripts

Cold DMs that actually book calls

The single thing every "AI agency" course teaches but rarely shows verbatim. Steal these. Rewrite to your voice. Send 20/day.

1. The "I built you something" DM (highest reply rate)

Hey [NAME] — saw [SPECIFIC THING about their business — recent launch, post, hire].

I built a quick prototype of an AI [LEAD-GEN BOT / INBOX TRIAGE / CONTENT REPURPOSER] for [their company] before reaching out. Took me 30 mins. Want me to send the Loom video showing what it does?

No pitch — just thought it'd be useful and figured I'd ask before spending more time on it.

2. The "specific observation" DM

Hi [NAME], noticed [your team is hiring 3 SDRs / your founder is spending Saturdays on client onboarding / your support reply time on Trustpilot is >48 hours].

We built an AI agent for [SIMILAR COMPANY] that [SPECIFIC OUTCOME — cut their inbox time 70%, qualifies leads overnight, etc].

Worth 15 mins to see if it'd help? Happy to share the loom if a call's overkill.

3. The "after the call" follow-up

Hey [NAME] — thanks for the call.

Recap of what we discussed:
- Outcome you want: [REPEAT THEIR EXACT WORDS]
- Build I'll deliver: [ONE LINE]
- Timeline: [X DAYS]
- Investment: [£X SETUP + £X/MO RETAINER]

I've attached a 1-page proposal. To kick off this week, just reply "yes" and I'll send the invoice + start tomorrow.

Cheers, [YOUR NAME]
Volume reality. 20 DMs/day → ~2-4 replies → ~1 call → ~1 client every 2 weeks for the first 60 days. After that, referrals start. The agency people online showing 10× this in week 1 are usually leaving out paid ads or warm networks.
