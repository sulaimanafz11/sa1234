Lesson 1

Anatomy of a visual prompt

A great image prompt has 6 ingredients in roughly this order:

  1. Subject — what's in the frame. Be specific. "A 60-year-old fisherman" beats "a man".
  2. Style — medium & aesthetic. "Cinematic photo, 35mm film grain, Roger Deakins lighting".
  3. Composition — framing & angle. "Low-angle close-up, rule of thirds, subject left".
  4. Lighting — direction, mood, temperature. "Golden hour rim-light, warm tungsten fill".
  5. Mood — emotional descriptor. "Contemplative, weathered, hopeful".
  6. Technical — lens, aspect ratio, camera. "85mm, f/1.8, shallow depth of field, --ar 16:9".

Negative prompts (what NOT to include) only matter for some models — see cheatsheets below.

Cheatsheets

Model-by-model quick reference

🎨 Midjourney v7

  • Prompts can be terse — MJ fills in style aggressively. Less is more.
  • Use --ar 16:9 / --ar 9:16 / --ar 1:1 for aspect ratio.
  • --style raw for photorealism (less artistic by default).
  • --v 7 sets the version. --s 250 stylization (0–1000).
  • Reference images: --cref [URL] for character, --sref [URL] for style.
  • Best at: posters, brand visuals, moodboards, anything painterly.

🍌 NanoBanana (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image)

  • Conversational. You can iterate: "now make it night-time", "add a coffee cup".
  • Strong character consistency across edits — same person, multiple scenes.
  • Free via Gemini app. API access for builders.
  • Less artistic than MJ but unbeatable for product shots, edits, and Photoshop-style fixes.
  • Best at: product mockups, character sheets, "fix this image" workflows.

⚡ Flux (Black Forest Labs)

  • Photoreal king. Best for human faces, hands, realistic skin.
  • Hosted free on Krea, fal.ai, Replicate. Or run locally.
  • Detailed prompts work well — Flux follows them more literally than MJ.
  • Fine-tuneable: train your own LoRA on a face, character, or style.
  • Best at: photoreal portraits, product photography, character training.

🎬 Sora · Veo · Runway · Kling

  • Sora (ChatGPT Pro) — best motion coherence at 10–20s. Realistic.
  • Veo 3 (Gemini Advanced) — best 1080p quality + audio sync.
  • Runway Gen-4 — creator-favourite. Motion brush, video-to-video, mature toolkit.
  • Kling — physics often look right, cheaper credits.
  • For all: structure prompts as "[Subject] [action]. [Camera movement]. [Setting + lighting]."
  • Always describe camera movement explicitly: "slow push-in", "orbit left", "static handheld".
Examples

Prompts that work — copy & adapt

Premium product shot (MJ / NanoBanana)

A luxury oud fragrance bottle on dark obsidian stone, single overhead spotlight, dramatic side shadows, slight haze in air, macro detail on the gold cap, deep amber reflections, cinematic still-life, 85mm, f/2.8, hyper-detailed --ar 1:1 --v 7 --style raw

Editorial portrait (Flux / MJ)

Portrait of a 60-year-old fisherman, weathered face, kind eyes, holding a worn rope, warm golden-hour light from camera-left, ocean blur in background, 35mm film aesthetic, Roger Deakins lighting, slight halation on highlights, photorealistic, shot on Kodak Portra 400 --ar 4:5

Brand hero image (MJ)

Editorial flat lay, modern minimalist, single ceramic cup of saffron tea on linen, scattered dried roses, soft window light from upper-left, muted earth tones, slight film grain, magazine-quality composition --ar 16:9 --style raw

Character consistency (NanoBanana)

Same character — a 30-year-old woman with curly red hair, freckles, wearing a green linen shirt — in a coffee shop, reading a book, soft afternoon light, candid photo style.

(For follow-ups, just say: "now in a park", "now at sunset", etc.)

Cinematic video (Sora / Veo)

A lone hooded figure walking across a snowy mountain ridge at dawn. Slow drone push-in from above, camera orbits gently to the right. Pink and orange morning light, fog drifting through valleys below. Cinematic, anamorphic lens flares, 24fps film look. 16:9.

Product video (Runway / Veo)

Macro shot of a perfume bottle on textured stone. Camera slowly orbits around the bottle, light glints across the gold cap. Particles of dust float through a single warm spotlight. Subtle smoke wisps. 9:16 vertical for Reels. 8 seconds.
Pro moves

Things that 10× your output

  • Reference images. MJ --cref and --sref are gold for consistent characters / brand styles.
  • Seeds. Same seed + tweaked prompt = controlled variations. Great for refining.
  • Aspect ratio first. Decide where it'll be used (post / story / reel / banner) before prompting.
  • Iterate, don't rewrite. If 80% is right, ask for a small tweak instead of starting over.
  • Use a prompt builder. Ask the AI tutor or ChatGPT to "turn this idea into a Midjourney prompt".
  • Lighting is the secret. Same subject, different lighting = different image. Always specify it.
