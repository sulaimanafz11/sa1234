You mentioned Dan Martell and Nate Herk — they're great, and there are 20+ more in the same league. This is the curated list, organised by what each creator is best at, with direct links. Skip the algorithm — go straight to the signal.

How to use this page. Pick the topic you care about right now (Building / Agents / Money / News / Engineering). Subscribe to 2-3 creators in that category. Watch one full video from each before adding anyone else. Following 50 AI creators = signal drowns in noise.

Strategy

How to actually use these without drowning

  1. Pick 1 creator per category. 5-6 creators total, max.
  2. Subscribe + bell on YouTube. No bell = algorithm decides. You decide.
  3. One newsletter only. The Rundown OR Ben's Bites — not both.
  4. Block 30 mins on Sunday for "AI catch-up". Watch saved videos / read newsletter backlog. Resist mid-week binges.
  5. Take notes in Notion / Obsidian. Tag by topic so you can find them later.
  6. Build something within a week of learning it. Otherwise it doesn't stick.
  7. Audit every 3 months. Unsubscribe from creators who've stopped teaching you new things.
Missing someone you trust? The list will grow as the space matures. For now, this is the curated short-list — already a 3-month learning curriculum if you actually watch + apply.
